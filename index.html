How it works

From a broken fan to a fixed one, in four steps.

  1. Say what needs fixing

    Pick a service, describe the problem, add photos, and choose a time and address.

  2. Get a quote first

    The worker sends a price before starting. Accept it, or decline and try someone else.

  3. Follow the job live

    See when your worker is on the way, has arrived and is working. Chat in the app any time.

  4. Pay and review

    Pay by cash, card or FixIt wallet, get a receipt, and rate the work.

Safety and trust

You know who is coming, and what it costs.

Letting a stranger into your home takes trust. FixIt is built so you can check before you book.

Every worker is checked
Workers submit their NIC and profile, and the FixIt team reviews each one before they can take a job.
The price is agreed up front
You see the worker's rate on their profile and get a quote for your job. Work starts only after you accept it.
Real ratings from real jobs
Only customers who completed a booking can leave a review. Workers earn Bronze, Silver and Gold levels from their record.
Free to cancel before the job starts
Plans change. Cancel or move the booking to another time from the app, at no charge, until work begins.
Help when something goes wrong
Message your worker in the booking chat, or report a problem to FixIt support from your account.

Questions

Good to know before you book.

How is the price decided?

Each worker lists a rate on their profile. After you send a request, the worker replies with a quote for your job. You only go ahead if you accept it.

How do I pay?

Choose cash, card or your FixIt wallet when the job is done. If you pay cash, hand it to the worker and they confirm it in the app. You get a receipt either way.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Yes. You can cancel for free until the job starts, or ask to move it to a different date and time.

What if a worker doesn't reply?

Requests that go unanswered expire on their own, and the app tells you so you can pick another worker.

Where is FixIt available?

Anywhere in Sri Lanka where workers have signed up. Workers set how far they travel, and the app shows you the ones who cover your address with their distance from you.

Do I need an account to look around?

No. You can browse workers as a guest. To book, sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code.
